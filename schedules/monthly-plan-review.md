# Monthly plan review

First of the month: see the shape of the whole, celebrate quietly, suggest one thing.

## Prompt

> Read progress.json and the plan folder. Tell me, warmly and briefly: how much is answered,
> what areas are untouched, and one focus area that would mean the most next. Celebrate
> quietly; no streaks, no scores. Offer to rebuild my plan if the month produced a lot.

## Notes

- If the plan has not been rebuilt in a while and the person is nearly done, offer the build
  and the print.
- Never suggest more than one focus area.
