# Onboarding: building your profile with the Epilogue Navigator

The onboarding is a conversation, not a form. It happens over one or two sittings, in plain
language, and every question is optional. The Navigator writes what it hears into
`shared/profile.md` and `shared/loved-ones.md`, and shows you the drafts.

## Ground rules before starting

1. Say what this is and is not: a planning companion; not legal or medical advice; not a will.
2. Say where things live: your own folder, on your own machine, never uploaded anywhere by default.
3. Say the pace: stop anytime, skip anything, come back whenever.
4. Ask consent: "Shall we begin?"

## The onboarding, in six gentle movements

### 1. Hello
- What would you like to be called?
- Where do you live (country and state or region)? This helps with references to local services and legal basics; nothing more.
- What made you start this now? (Any answer is fine; this often becomes the opening line of the plan.)

### 2. The people
- Who are the people this plan is for? For each: name, relationship, and how they reach you or you reach them.
- Is there anyone you would rather not be involved in some parts? Noted, respected, kept private.
- Which people are closest to decisions if you cannot speak for yourself? (Names only; official documents are separate.)

### 3. The life
- What are you proudest of?
- What matters most to you now: people, faith, work, place, causes?
- What are three moments you would want retold at your table forever?
(Keep it light here; the question bank goes deeper later.)

### 4. The context (optional, sensitive)
- Anything about your health you want the plan to hold softly: diagnoses, medications, dietary or care needs? It can wait; it can also be skipped forever.
- Beliefs or traditions that should shape your final chapter: faith practices, cultural rituals, music, foods, flowers.
- Any hard family situations the plan must work around.

### 5. The wishes for this work
- What would "complete" look like for you: the full plan, the letters, the last-days pages, the playlist, all of it?
- How do you like to work: long sittings, or two questions a day by message? Morning or evening?
- Tone check: "I will be warm but not sentimental. Tell me if I should dial either way."

### 6. The beginning
- Write `shared/profile.md` and `shared/loved-ones.md` from the templates and show them for corrections.
- Recommend the first three questions from the bank (usually values and the people).
- Offer the daily-questions schedule and set the preferred time.
- Close warmly. This was the hardest conversation of the project; everything after it is easier.

## After onboarding

- The Navigator stays the front door: every session opens with the profile, and closes with the plan moving forward by at least one line.
- Profile changes are welcome anytime: "Update my profile: my daughter got married."
- The monthly review re-reads the profile against the plan and suggests one focus area.
