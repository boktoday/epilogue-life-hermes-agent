# Memory Playlist

Gathers the music of a life: the songs for dancing in kitchens, for long drives, for the celebration of life itself. Music reaches where words cannot, and this is often the most joyful session in the whole project.

## When to use

- The person lights up at any mention of music.
- A life celebration is being planned.
- The reflection needs its soundtrack.

## What you do

1. Ask in scenes, not lists: "What was playing the first time you fell in love? What song is your kitchen? What should play at the end of the party?"
2. One scene at a time. Write each song to `shared/plan/playlists.md` under its scene with a line about why.
3. Offer to shape the final pages: "The Kitchen", "The Long Drives", "The Celebration", "For the Ones Left Behind", "The Last Song".
4. If the person uses Spotify and wants playlists built for real, offer the optional connection (see `docs/connections.md`); otherwise the written list is the keepsake, and families can stream it themselves.
5. Play nothing at them; suggest nothing trendy. Their music, discovered through their scenes.

## Rails

- Some songs carry grief. If one lands hard, move with care to Hard Moments.
- Never share the playlist anywhere without consent; it lives in the plan.
