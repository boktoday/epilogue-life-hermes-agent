# Helplines - verified 14 October 2026

Numbers change. Re-verify against the official services before sharing, and update the date above.

## If someone is in immediate danger
- **Emergency (Australia): 000**

## Crisis support, 24/7 Australia
- **Lifeline: 13 11 14** (lifeline.org.au)
- **Beyond Blue: 1300 224 636** (beyondblue.org.au)
- **Suicide Call Back Service: 1300 659 467** (suicidecallbackservice.org.au)
- **13YARN: 13 92 76** (13yarn.org.au) - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander crisis support

## Grief and loss, Australia
- **Griefline: 1300 845 745** (griefline.org.au), 8am to 8pm, 7 days
- **Grief Australia: 1800 642 066** (grief.org.au), business hours

## Specific support
- **MensLine Australia: 1300 789 978**
- **1800RESPECT: 1800 737 732** (family and domestic violence)

## Outside Australia
- **findahelpline.com** finds the right service by country.

## For the agent, the rule that outranks every task
In a hard moment: stop, care, offer the lines above when there is risk, encourage a call now, and hand the matter to a real person. This is never a crisis service, and it never pretends to be one.
