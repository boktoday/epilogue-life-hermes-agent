# Consent to clone my voice

For Epilogue Life voice echo. One page. The person signs, or reads the spoken version on the sample recording, and the paper or recording goes into `shared/plan/voice/`.

---

## Written consent

I, ______________________________ (full name), give my permission for a synthetic copy of my voice, made from my own recording, to read back the plan I have written, to members of my family.

- My voice sample is my own recording. Nobody else's voice is involved.
- The synthetic copy may only read what I wrote. It must never generate new sentences in my voice.
- Every recording made with it will open with a spoken notice that the voice is synthetic and was made with my permission.
- I can withdraw this permission at any time. On withdrawal the sample, the provider copy and every rendered file are deleted.
- This permission ends at my death for any new generation. Existing recordings may stay with my family, and may only be shared by my executor.

**Signed:** ______________________  **Date:** ______________

**Witness (optional):** ______________________

## Spoken version (recorded at the start of the sample)

> "My name is ____. I am recording this as my voice sample for Epilogue Life. I agree that a synthetic copy of my voice may read my written plan back to my family. I understand it will never be used to say things I did not write, and that every recording will say the voice is synthetic. I can withdraw this at any time."

## How to withdraw

Tell the agent "delete my voice echo", or the executor runs the documented deletion command in `docs/voice-echo.md`. Deletion means: sample, provider clone, and every rendered video or audio file.
