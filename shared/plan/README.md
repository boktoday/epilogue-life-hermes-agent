# Your plan folder

This is where the output lives, built by the Plan Builder and grown over time:

- `EPILOGUE-LIFE-PLAN.md` - the complete plan, in markdown
- `epilogue-life-plan.html` - the printable, beautiful version for sharing
- `reflection.md` - your life reflection, woven from your answers
- `letters/` - one file per person for Wish I Had Said
- `last-days.md` - comfort and dignity preferences for the final chapter
- `playlists.md` - the soundtrack of your life
- `shared-log.md` - a record of what was shared with whom, when, and with what consent

Nothing here is ever sent anywhere without your explicit, in-the-moment yes.
