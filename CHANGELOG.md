# Changelog

## v1.0.0 - 14 October 2026
- First Hermes edition of Epilogue Life.
- Nine skills: Navigator, Life Questions, Reflection Writer, Wish I Had Said, Last Days Planner, Memory Playlist, Plan Builder, Share The Plan, Hard Moments.
- Question bank ported faithfully from the web app (35 questions, seven areas).
- Plan folder system (profile, loved ones, answers, plan) with setup script.
- Three schedules: daily questions, weekly reflection, monthly review.
- Email sharing flow through Maton (Google Mail), print-first everywhere.
- Crisis and grief rails with verified Australian helplines and a global finder.
- PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license.
