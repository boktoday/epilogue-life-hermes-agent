If you are hearing this, I recorded it on purpose, while my voice was still my own. A few things I want you to know. First, I have no regrets about the ordinary Sundays. The beach, the burnt sausages, the arguments about the radio. That was the good stuff. Keep the house if it gives you comfort. Sell it without guilt if it does not. Look after each other, and ring the person you keep meaning to ring. And when you think of me, do not do it quietly. Tell the bad jokes. Make the tea too strong. I love you, all of you, exactly as you are.
